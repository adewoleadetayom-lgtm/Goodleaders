Give Your Life to Jesus ❤️

If you sincerely want to begin following Jesus, you can pray to God in your own words.

Here is a simple prayer you can use:

Say this:
Lord Jesus,

I believe that You are the Son of God.

I believe that You died for my sins and that God raised You from the dead.

I am sorry for my sins, and I ask You to forgive me.

Today, I choose to follow You.

Come into my life and be my Lord and Savior.

Help me to live according to Your will, love others, and grow in Your Word.

Thank You for loving me and giving me a new beginning.

In Jesus' name,

Amen.
